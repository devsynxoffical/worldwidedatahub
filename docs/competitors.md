# Competitor analysis v1 (25 Nov 2026)

Method: Google results (US) for four target searches: "germany business email list buy",
"saudi arabia mobile number list database", "buy b2b email database by country", and
"uk business email list database for sale". This is a first pass from search results. A backlink
and keyword-overlap comparison needs Ahrefs or Semrush (optional; Windsor.ai can connect either).

## Findings that change our priorities

1. **Buyers expect a sample, the price and the record count on the page.** Our pages show the price,
   and the record count appears next to the title. The FAQ now repeats these. Next: put "Request a
   free sample" right next to "Add to cart" on every product (already there), and consider naming
   a fixed sample size.
2. **Country, then city and industry pages win long-tail searches.** Our country hubs (8) are the
   first layer. Industry pages should only be built for databases we really have.
3. **Compliance content is a ranking and trust signal.** Competitors rank guides about the legality
   of buying lists. Our blog topics 8 (GDPR, PECR, CAN-SPAM) and 7 (buying mobile lists by country)
   go up the queue.
4. **Many competitor results are platform pages** (datahub.io, github.io, issuu, slideshare), not
   their own domains. We will not do this. It's risky under Google's site-reputation abuse policy,
   and it builds no value on our own domain.
